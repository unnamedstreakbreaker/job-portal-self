How the scores work

  • Genuineness % (out of 100): direct official careers/ATS link 30 (company careers home only: 15) · established company 20 · posted in last 30 days 15 (31–45 days: 8) · seen on an official page or 2+ sources 15 · no red flags 20. It measures how trustworthy the listing is — not your chance of selection.
  • Match %: share of your skills the job asks for (70%) plus how your experience fits its range (30%). Skills only or experience only also work.

Stay safe

  • Real companies never charge a fee for interviews, training, laptops or "registration".
  • HR emails from Gmail/Yahoo/Outlook claiming to be TCS, Infosys, Amazon etc. are a scam signal — check the domain.
  • Big companies take applications only on their careers site, so most cards show "HR email not published". That is normal.
